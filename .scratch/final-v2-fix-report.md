## Finding 1 — fixed: booking/media/recording isolation
`MeetingRoomMedia` is the single naming/parser helper: `{MediaNamespace}-{logicalKey}-{meetingId:N}`. Physical five Hall room keys/capacities remain unchanged. Tokens, participant lists, video-source updates, metadata, Egress composites, chat relay/system lines, booking cleanup and recording viewer sampling address booking-specific rooms. Legacy `hall-{key}` does not parse and is never removed/rewritten. An A token (including a refreshed LiveKit token) cannot enter B's media room. At takeover everyone leaves A's media session, including a person invited to both; they must join B.

`BookingMediaSession` resolves ownership before and after the media listing. `RecordingBoundary.EndPreviousAsync` runs before next join/recording start and from both workers. Accepted stop is insufficient: it checks Egress's room association and terminal status after Stop. Unknown/missing room, unconfigured Egress, stop failure or still-active/ending status blocks next admission. Recording/Processing rows of previous bookings are checked. Foreign namespace Egress in copied QA rows is quarantined, never stopped or finalized by that environment. `LiveKitEgressInfo.Room` reads LiveKit `room_name`; old mocks were updated to provide actual room association. `ILiveKitRoomAdmin.ListRoomsAsync` discovers stale sessions and filters the helper parser before participant/admin calls.

Chat association, recording-chat queries, card/metadata recording state and NoteJoin match MeetingId. Recording start and worker viewer sampling also require role eligibility within that booking. No cross-booking guest/viewer grants. Existing historical mixed recordings are not repairable by these filters: audio/video already captured remains a release/operator concern; the fix prevents new mixing and filters mismatched chat. No retroactive viewer cleanup was attempted without authoritative historic presence data.

## Finding 2 — fixed: persistent monotonic takeover high-water
New immutable `MeetingRoomOwnership` ledger stores RoomKey, MeetingId, WindowStartAt high-water and ClaimedAt; no FK to mutable/deleted Meetings. Unique room+booking claim prevents a predecessor claiming again. Resolution refuses a previously superseded booking even before its original EndAt. An out-of-order older request cannot roll the high-water back. A genuinely new Use-now booking after cancellation may claim the slot; its high-water advances even if its floored start precedes the old early window. All ownership writes are serialized per room locally and with a PostgreSQL transaction-scoped advisory lock when this method owns the transaction. Existing outer transactions must maintain the same serialization if this helper is introduced inside such a transaction; current production callers do not wrap it in one.

Organizer update/cancel claims the original room before changing Location/time/status, so move/future reschedule/cancel cannot erase an already-open takeover even without a prior worker/list observation. Restart/new DbContext retains ownership. Ledger is independent of mutable rows; not a historical Location-only query.

Migration `20261012204540_MeetingRoomOwnershipLedger` generated using isolated dotnet-ef (10.0.0; runtime 10.0.12 warned about tool version). Inspected Up: only ledger CreateTable + unique CreateIndex; Down drops that table; snapshot adds only this entity. No migration applied. Do not drop the ledger while v2 serves traffic or predecessors may revive.

Upload-derived paths use the same guard before upload/delete-race cleanup.
- `MeetingRooms:SpoolDir`, `CacheDir`: use separate QA roots. Local paths must be strict descendants, no drive/UNC/traversal/root escape.
