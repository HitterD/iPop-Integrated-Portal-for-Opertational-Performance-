iPop mobile app · v2 — every module

Part 2 — the rest of iPop on a phone, in the same style as part 1. Same tab bar (Chat · Mail · Calls · Calendar · More), large titles, ‹ back for detail screens, bottom sheets for forms and actions, one floating button per page, tap targets ≥ 44 px. Directory is redesigned first; then Projects, Tickets, News & Memos, Notifications, Files, the extra Calls / Calendar / Chat / Mail screens, Profile & Settings, global search, Admin (admins only) and Sign in. Employee numbers and positions appear only on the Admin screens.

Directory

People first: one search for name, department and site, favourites on top, A–Z list with a section index, and quick Chat / Call on every row. Departments become a site-by-site tree. Users never see employee numbers or positions.

Projects

Cards for the project list, a one-column-at-a-time board, a full-screen task with checklist and comments, and a simplified Reports tab. Overview, List, Timeline, Calendar, Workload stay as view tabs.

Tickets

ICT tickets synced with iDesk: list grouped by what needs you, a guided new-ticket sheet, and a detail screen that reads like a chat.

News & Memos

Magazine home with one hero and compact rows, a readable article page (17 px body, full-width images), the calm condolence style, and a full-screen editor.

Notifications

One list with module icons and unread dots, swipe for Mark read / Snooze, and preferences as a normal grouped settings page instead of a dialog.

Files

Finder-style: Locations first (one per NAS site), then folders as a list with icons or thumbnails, preview full screen, actions in a sheet.

Calls & Meetings · extras

In-call screen for voice group calls, the pill when you minimise it, a meeting room on a phone, and the booking sheet with a horizontally scrolling timeline.

Calendar · extras

Event detail as its own screen and a full-screen new-event form that starts with the four meeting kinds.

Chat · extras

Group info, the attach sheet and the long-press menu — the three things most often hidden behind small icons on desktop.

Mail · extras

Full-screen compose with recipient chips and the address book, and the Mailboxes & labels sheet.

Profile, settings & search

Your profile (photo, email and extension only — HR data is read-only by office rule), one Settings list, and the global search that replaces Ctrl K.

Admin Admin only

Only for admins (More › Admin settings). The real sections from /admin: Users · Organization · Security · Meeting rooms · Announcements · Holidays · HRIS sync. Admins may see Employee IDs (NIK) here — nowhere else.

Sign in & first run

Brand-first sign-in with Employee ID and password, an Install iPop hint when opened in a browser, and three short onboarding cards after the first sign-in.
