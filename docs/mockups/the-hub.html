How a topic works

Every topic goes New → Ongoing → Solved. Most of the time it replaces a meeting.

1

Open a topic New

Press New topic. Pick what it is (a problem, a question that needs a clear answer, an idea), write what you need from the others and by when. For big purchases and product changes, iPop prepares the topic for you.

2

Invite the right people first

Add at least 1 person from another department; 2 people is enough. iPop suggests who usually helps with this kind of topic. Send stays off until someone from another department is in, because the point is to work across departments.

3

Everyone is told

Each person gets a popup on PC and phone. Only invited people can open the topic. Forgot someone? Add them later; they see the whole history.

4

Talk it through, with files Ongoing

The topic becomes Ongoing as soon as someone replies. Write like a chat, attach quotes, Excel or photos. When you need an answer from one person, pick Needs an answer from. iPop reminds them after 2 working days. After 3, their department head gets a reminder, without the topic details.

5

Mark as solved Solved

When the problem is solved, anyone in the topic presses Mark as solved and writes the answer in one or two sentences. If there is still follow-up work, it goes to Projects with a name and a date. Solved by mistake? Press Reopen.

Common questions

Why can't I send to only my own department?The Hub is for work across departments. For your own team, use Chat.
Who can read my topic?Only the people you invite. Directors and department heads cannot open it unless you add them. Overview shows them numbers only, never the topic.
Is this instead of meetings?For most questions, yes. If a topic really needs a meeting, press Schedule a meeting inside it and the people are already there.
What if nobody answers?Reminder after 2 working days. After 3, their department head gets a reminder that they have a late answer, without the topic details.
Can I invite my head or a director?Yes. Add them like anyone else when the topic needs them. Once added, they can read the whole topic.
