Inbox — what needs you sits on top

A summary line says what is waiting. Needs action (approvals, leaders, meetings starting) is pinned in its own box. Everything else follows by day. For your info rows are quieter: smaller, lighter and never a popup. One row per ticket, chat or thread, with “3 updates” instead of three rows.

Three tiers — only what matters interrupts

Needs action gets the full popup that stays until handled, with sound, and still shows during Busy or full screen. Messages are a small toast that slides away after 8 seconds and stays in the Inbox. For your info never pops up: it only grows the grey count on the tray icon.

Popups with files — Preview or Download straight away

Every file gets a card with type, size and page count, plus Preview and Download buttons. Pictures show inline. Maximizing the popup shows bigger cards with page-1 thumbnails. Preview opens the viewer (screen 5), and downloads go to Downloads.

Preview rules: PDF, pictures, video and audio open directly. Word and PowerPoint are converted to PDF on the iPop server (the same engine as Notes) and cached. Excel opens in a sheet viewer. ZIP, .ppt and other types show “download only”.
Access is the same as in iPop: if you cannot open it on the web, you cannot open it here.
Sources: mail, chat and iDesk ticket attachments, and Projects/Notes files attached to the notification.

File viewer — its own window, ← → between files

Opens from a popup or the Inbox. You can move through every file of the message with ← and →, download to Downloads, or open the file in its Windows app. After a download a bar offers Show in folder and Open.
