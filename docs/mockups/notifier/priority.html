Three tiers — only what matters interrupts

Needs action gets the full popup with a gentle ring that stays until handled, and it still shows during Busy or full screen. Messages are a small toast that slides away after 8 seconds. Hover it to keep it, and it stays in the Inbox either way. For your info never pops up: it only grows the grey count on the tray icon.

File viewer — its own window, ← → between files

Opens from a popup or the Inbox. Move through every file of the message with ← and → or the list on the right, zoom in and out, download to Downloads, or open the file in its Windows app. Word and PowerPoint show a short “Preparing preview” the first time.
