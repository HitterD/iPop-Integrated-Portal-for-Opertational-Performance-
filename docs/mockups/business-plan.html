Common questions

Are the project tasks made automatically?Yes. Each kind of change has its own task list (Admin can edit it in Rules & data). When you send, iPop makes the tasks and gives each one to the person who has that role in the product team. In step 5 of the form you can untick tasks that don't apply, or add your own.
Can I add a task later?Yes. In the project press New task or + Add task under a column. A short form asks what, who (pick a role, iPop shows the person), when, and what it waits for.
Who picks the people?Each department head sets their person for each brand once. Every change for that brand uses the same team.
Where do I see my tasks?In Projects with Filter: only mine, in My tasks on the Dashboard, and on your Calendar. Answers that need one click also pop up on your PC and phone.

Why this module

Similar cases in other companies, and how they prevent them.

Catatan mockup: halaman penjelasan untuk presentasi ke GM, bukan tab harian. Isinya pola kejadian serupa di manufaktur/FMCG, cara industri mencegahnya, dan bagian modul yang menjawabnya. Disusun dari praktik umum S&OP, PLM, dan CapEx governance.

What happens

Packaging bought at the minimum order, then the product stops

Printed film, cartons and labels have big minimum orders and fit one product only. When it stops, what's left is written off.

How others prevent it
  • An official change notice every affected team must answer
  • Stop date planned from leftover materials
  • Bonus packs or bundles to use stock faster
In iPop

Product changes + When to stop, which shows the leftover in Rupiah for each date.

What happens

Teams buy without knowing Marketing's plan

PPIC and Purchasing buy on the old forecast while Marketing already plans a change. Each is right with their own data.

How others prevent it
  • S&OP: one monthly meeting, one plan for every team
  • Long purchases need a product-life check
In iPop

Before you buy + the monthly product meeting with its agenda built for you.

What happens

Supplier minimums force 3–5 years of stock

One big order gets a lower unit price. If the product changes, the saving per piece is smaller than the loss on leftovers.

How others prevent it
  • Blanket orders: minimum-order price, delivered in parts
  • Supplier buy-back or consignment
  • Plain packaging plus a label for small-volume products
In iPop

The answer "Not sure, buy in smaller deliveries" and the choices in When to stop.

What happens

A machine is bought, then sits idle

A special machine is bought for one product or format. The product stops or shrinks, and the machine keeps costing money.

How others prevent it
  • Each machine purchase names its products and how long
  • Check real use 12–24 months after installing
  • Plan which product moves onto the line before one stops
In iPop

A check for every machine, the What depends on it list, and a 24-month review.

What happens

Land or a long lease goes unused

A 5-year contract or land for an expansion that doesn't happen keeps costing money for more than 3 years.

How others prevent it
  • Contracts with options (3 + 2), not a fixed term
  • Marketing confirms volume before signing
In iPop

A check for land or leases longer than 3 years.

What happens

New design or new label rules

A new logo, claim or label rule means old packaging can't be used after a certain date.

How others prevent it
  • New design starts when the old stock runs out
  • If a rule sets the date, buy less of the old design early
In iPop

The New pack design change uses the same When to stop tool.
