Q1 C · Q2 C · Q7 · Q9 B

Home — a front page, not a mailbox

  • Featured: the newest pinned or announcement post, big. Video posts play from here. Condolences are never featured.
  • Three smaller stories beside it; then a Latest grid.
  • Posts without a picture get a category illustration (icon on the category colour) instead of an empty box.
  • Filters are one row of tabs: For you · Unread · To acknowledge (red count) · Saved, then category chips. "Drafts" only for people who can post.
  • The red strip appears only when something needs acknowledgement.

Q1 C

Reading — two columns

  • Opening a story switches to list + article; ↑ ↓ (or J / K) jumps to the next post. "All news" goes back to the front page.
  • The article uses a reading width (~680px) and a serif body, like Apple News.
  • Acknowledgement is a clear card with the deadline and progress, instead of a small button.
  • Save, copy link, mark unread, and manager actions (edit, pin, remind, delete) live in the top bar / ⋯ menu.
