Stage 1

Other people's calendars

  • Other calendars in the sidebar: add anyone with the people picker. Each person gets a colour and a checkbox. The list is saved to your account.
  • What you see depends on each event's visibility:
    • Public: title and location.
    • Busy only / Private: a striped "Busy" block, no title.
    • Meetings not answered yet: dashed "Tentative".
  • Only personal events and meetings count as busy. Tasks, scheduled mail and News never appear on other people's calendars. Declined meetings don't appear.
  • Click a block to see the popover (shown on Rina's Tue 13:00).

Stage 1

Visibility per event + Find a time

  • Every event and meeting has the three-way visibility choice. The preselected value is your default from Settings (stage 4), initially Busy only.
  • Invited people always see the full meeting, whatever the visibility.
  • Find a time shows each invitee's busy and tentative blocks for the chosen day and suggests slots free for everyone in working hours (08:00–17:00, project holidays excluded). Click a suggestion to move the meeting.
  • Existing events become Busy only on rollout, so no old title is suddenly public.
