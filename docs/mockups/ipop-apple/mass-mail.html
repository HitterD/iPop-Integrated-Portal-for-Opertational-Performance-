Composer

  • A group is one chip with its count; click it to peek or expand to individuals.
  • The recipient count is computed by the server, after removing duplicates across To/Cc/Bcc and groups.
  • Optional Hide recipient list, like Bcc.
  • The 1,001st recipient blocks Send with a clear message instead of "too_many_recipients".

Confirmation

  • Shown when there are more than 50 recipients, on Send, Schedule and Forward.

  • The numbers come from the server (the same calculation used for sending).
