🧩 Whiteboard: own canvas

A whiteboard that can replace ClickUp

iPop draws the board itself now (no Excalidraw). Clean shapes like ClickUp, only the pen looks hand-drawn. The board fills the page and goes full screen. Everything here is a real element of the board, stored with the page, so it syncs live and shows up in Version history.

Try it: the board in section 1 works. Pick a tool, draw, drag, double-click a sticky to write in it. Tick Looks good or write what to change under each section, then press Copy all feedback at the bottom.

Shapes snap to a light grid and show blue guide lines when they line up with another shape. Mouse wheel scrolls, Ctrl + wheel zooms, space + drag moves the board.

In the toolbar

  • Select, Hand, Pen, Highlighter, Eraser
  • Sticky note (6 colours)
  • Text, shapes (rectangle, rounded, circle, diamond, triangle, star, cloud, block arrow, cylinder, speech bubble)
  • Connector: straight, elbow or curved, sticks to the side of a shape
  • Frame, Image or file, Comment, Laser

Quick menu on a selection

Colour, fill, line, font (iPop, Handwriting, Mono), size, layer, lock, duplicate, delete. Stickies also get Make task.

Old boards

Converted automatically. Shapes become neat, pen strokes stay exactly as drawn, old text keeps its handwriting font. Each board first gets a named version "Before the new canvas".

Working at the same time

Package 2

Everyone on the board sees each other's cursor with their name. What someone is holding gets their colour. Click a face top-right to Follow: your screen moves with theirs until you move yourself. The laser (L) leaves a short red trail everyone sees, for pointing during a meeting. In section 1, Rina is moving around: click her face to follow her.

Follow

A thin coloured edge shows you are following someone. Moving the board yourself, or pressing Esc, stops it.

Templates and library

Package 2Package 1

Templates open as a picture gallery (toolbar button, and in the middle of an empty board). Eleven starters plus your own boards saved with "Save as template". My library keeps shapes and groups you reuse, saved on the server, so it follows you to any PC. The old Excalidraw library in your browser is brought over the first time.

Templates

Retro, Flowchart, Brainstorm (today) + Mind map, Kanban, SWOT, Timeline, User journey, Fishbone, Meeting agenda, Org chart. A template is added beside what is already on the board, never over it.

Library

My library is only yours. Team library is shared with everyone who can open the board.

Comments, voting and mind maps

Package 3

Comments (C) pin to a spot or a shape and use the same threads as Notes: @mention, reactions, resolve. Voting: anyone who can edit starts a round; everyone gets 3 votes (you choose), votes stay hidden until it ends, then the stickies can be sorted by votes. Mind map: select a box, Tab adds a branch, Enter a sibling. Try voting on the board in section 1.
