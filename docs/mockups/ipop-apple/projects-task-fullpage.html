Try it below. Click a card to open the quick view. Click ⤢ (or double-click a card) and the same sheet grows into the full page: the board steps back, the title glides into place, and the sections settle in one after another. Back or Esc shrinks it back into the quick view. ↑/↓ moves to the previous/next task.
