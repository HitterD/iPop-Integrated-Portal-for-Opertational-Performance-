One click each side

Ask → Allow

  • Clicking someone's Busy block explains why it's grey, and offers Ask to see details (max once a day per person).
  • The owner gets a notification with Allow titles right in it — or picks a different level, or ignores it. Nobody is told about an ignore.

  • Nobody is notified when someone just looks — they only see busy times.
