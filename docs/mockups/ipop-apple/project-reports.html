What is Reports for?

One question: how is this project tracking?

  • Tiles answer "how far, how late, how fast" at a glance.
  • Milestones are real checkpoints a PM creates — no auto-generated filler.
  • Burndown shows if we will finish in time; Velocity shows team pace.

Use a template

Templates live where projects begin

  • "Start from" toggles Blank / Template; the list collapses smoothly.
  • Built-in first, then templates saved by your team. A summary line shows exactly what will be created.
  • Milestone dates are relative and shift to the chosen start date.
Make a template

⋯ → Save as template…

  • Click the menu item: a sheet lets you choose what to include (tasks, milestones, assignees, comments) and who can use it.
  • The same ⋯ menu appears on the Reports artboard header.

New milestone

A checkpoint that groups tasks — e.g. "UAT ready" or "Go-live Karawang". It's achieved when you mark it, or automatically when every linked task is done.
