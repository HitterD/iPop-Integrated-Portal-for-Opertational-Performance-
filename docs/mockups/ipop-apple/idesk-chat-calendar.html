iDesk items are real calendar events

  • Zoom bookings made in iDesk land on the calendar of the booker and every invited colleague.
  • Installation visits land on the requester's calendar at the chosen slot (1 hour).
  • Light-blue with a small iDesk mark. They count as busy, block Find a time and get the usual reminders.

  • Other tickets keep the dashed Ticket due marker — not busy, only visible to you.
