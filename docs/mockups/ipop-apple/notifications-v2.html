Notifications: today's layout, refined (v2). Click the rows and the categories, they work.

The layout stays the same as today: categories on the left (top to bottom), the list in the middle, details on the right. Only the details are refined:

  • Clear icons: every module has its own icon and colour (Chat, Mail, iDesk, Meetings…), so items no longer all show the grey shield.
  • Bigger text for 40+ users, and unread items are easy to spot (bold title + green dot + green time).
  • Chat history now exists: one row per conversation ("3 new messages"). Click it and the right side shows the last messages plus a reply box.
  • The right side follows the item type: chat shows messages + Reply, iDesk shows the ticket status + Reply in ticket, a meeting shows Join/Accept, a system item shows the plain message. The From/Priority/Status table is replaced by details that actually help.
  • Buttons at the top of the details have labels (Mark unread, Snooze, Delete), not just icons.
