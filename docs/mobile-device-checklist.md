# iPop on a real phone — 10-minute check

Open **https://ipop.santos.co.id** in Chrome on Android (or Safari on iPhone) and sign in.
Tick each line; note anything that feels slow, jumpy or wrong.

1. **Tab bar** — tap Chat, Mail, Calls, Calendar: the icon bounces a little and the phone gives a short tick.
2. **More (⋯)** — tap every tile (Dashboard, Directory, Notes, …): each opens its page; Back returns.
3. **Pages slide** — open a chat, a mail, a note: the screen slides in from the right; ‹ Back slides back.
4. **Pull to refresh** — on the Chat list, Mail, Notifications, Tickets and News: pull down from the top
   and let go. A green spinner turns, then the list refreshes.
5. **Swipe a notification** — Notifications: swipe a row left/right; the action icon grows and the phone ticks.
6. **Sheets** — tap ⋯ More or + in a chat: the sheet springs up; drag its handle down to close it.
7. **Upload** — in a chat: + → Gallery (pick a photo), + → Camera (take one), + → Document (a PDF).
   In Mail: new mail → paperclip and photo. In a Note: type "/image". Each shows the file; nothing hangs.
8. **Incoming call** — ask a colleague to call you while iPop is open: a full green screen with
   Decline / Message / Accept, the phone vibrates. Accept and talk; end the call.
9. **Toasts** — after sending, a small card slides up above the tab bar; the green button moves up out of its way.
10. **Settings → Vibration** — turn it off: taps no longer tick. Turn it back on.

Known limits: with iPop closed or the phone locked, a call shows as a normal notification, not the
full call screen (that needs the native app). iPhone HEIC photos upload as files; to get previews,
set iPhone Camera › Formats › Most Compatible.
